# Evidence behind the AI's programming rules

The plan and routine prompts (`guide()` in `ai.js`) follow these sources. The brief was compiled 2026-10-12 from sources that were actually fetched and checked. Where the evidence is weak, the prompt words it as a convention.

| Rule in the prompt | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| ~10–16 hard sets per muscle per week (beginners 6–10); secondary muscles count half | ACSM 2026 [1] (≥10 sets/week, diminishing returns past ~18–20); Schoenfeld 2017 [3]; Pelland 2024 preprint [4] | Moderate–high that more helps; the exact optimum is weak |
| Each muscle about twice a week; split by days available | ACSM 2026 [1]; Schoenfeld 2019 [7] (frequency matters little once volume is equal, so it's mainly a way to spread volume) | Moderate |
| Rest: heavy compounds 2–3 min, other compounds 90–120 s, isolation 60–90 s; time-efficient pace shorter, with opposing-muscle supersets | Singer 2024 [5] (small benefit beyond 60 s, little beyond 90 s for hypertrophy); Grgic 2018 [8] (trained lifters need >2 min for maximal strength); Robbins 2010 [10] (agonist–antagonist pairing) | Moderate; small effects |
| Most sets 1–3 reps short of failure; no failure on heavy barbell lifts | ACSM 2026 [1]; Refalo 2023 [11]; Robinson 2024 [12] | Moderate; exact reps-in-reserve targets are uncertain |
| Heaviest compound first, then compounds, then isolation | Nunes 2021 [13] (order affects strength gains, not hypertrophy) | Moderate |
| Full range of motion, long-muscle-length exercises | ACSM 2026 [1]; Wolf 2023 [15]; Kassiano 2023 [16] | Promising, not settled |
| Keep main lifts all month, rotate accessories every week or two | Exercise variation systematic review 2022 [17]; mostly convention | Weak |
| Week 4 deload: about half the sets, ~10% lighter, 3+ reps in reserve | Bell 2023 Delphi consensus [18]; Coleman 2024 [19] | Weak (expert consensus) |
| Fat loss: lift to keep muscle while dieting, with moderate volume | Resistance training during dieting preserves lean mass [20]; WHO 2020 [21] | Moderate |
