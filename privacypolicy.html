HypertroPhy privacy

HypertroPhy is a personal lifting log. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics and no HypertroPhy servers.

Health data

If you allow it, the Android app reads your weight from Health Connect to show your body-weight trend, suggest starting weights and estimate calories. It never writes to Health Connect and reads nothing else.

AI features

When you use the coach or the plan builder, the app sends your goal, training history and, if you've entered them, your sex, age, height and weight to the AI service you chose in Settings (for example OmniRoute or Google Gemini). Nothing is sent until you use those features.

Backups

Backup files contain your workouts, routines and measurements, and go wherever you choose to save them. AI keys are never included.
